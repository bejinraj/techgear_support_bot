# TechGear Product FAQ

## Order & Shipping

- Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Track via techgear.com/track or confirmation email link.
- Orders can be changed/cancelled only within 1 hour of placing via 'My Orders' page.

## Returns & Exchanges

- Returns allowed within 30 days of delivery, unused in original packaging.
- Size/color exchanges are free; other returns incur $5 shipping fee deducted from refund.
- Refunds processed within 5-7 business days post-inspection.

## Warranty

- 1-year manufacturer warranty covering defects. Excludes accidental, water damage, or normal wear.
- Claim warranty at techgear.com/warranty with order number.

## Account & Login

- Password resets via 'Forgot Password' link. Email changed under Account Settings > Profile.

# TechGear Troubleshooting Guide (techgear_troubleshooting.md)

## Product Won't Power On

1. Charge device for at least 30 minutes and retry.
2. Try a different charging cable/adapter.
3. Hold power button for 15 seconds to force restart.
4. If still unresolved, this is a hardware issue - direct customer to warranty claim.

## Device Won't Connect to Wi-Fi/Bluetooth

1. Restart device and connecting phone/router.
2. Confirm software is updated (Settings > About > Update).
3. Forget network/device pairing and reconnect.
4. If issue persists, escalate to technical support.

## App Crashes or Freezes

1. Force-close and reopen app.
2. Confirm app is updated from app store.
3. Restart device.
4. If crashes continue, request device model & app version, then escalate to tech support.

## Common Error Scenarios

- Error Code E01: Battery calibration - drain battery completely, then charge to 100% uninterrupted.
- Error Code E02: Firmware corruption - customer MUST NOT reinstall manually; escalate directly to tech support.
- Error Code E03: Sensor malfunction - cannot be fixed remotely; escalate to warranty/repair team.
